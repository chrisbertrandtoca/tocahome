## 🏗️ Technical Stack (The "Modern" Home)

We have moved away from traditional building materials in favor of more **Agile** solutions:

* **Structural Support:** 35% load-bearing wallpaper and a collection of "Live, Laugh, Love" signs.
* **HVAC:** A single USB-powered fan and the chilling realization of the 7.5% mortgage rate.
* **Security:** A "Beware of Dog" sign. We don't have a dog, but we have a recording of a Great Dane on a 4-hour loop.
* **Smart Home Integration:** The lights flicker whenever the neighbor uses their toaster. It’s not a wiring issue; it's *ambient notification technology*.
